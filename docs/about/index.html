About

Testing isour business.

Since 2012, Q-Leap has advised, trained and supported organisations in software quality from Luxembourg.

Our work

From a specification to the people using the software.

Our testers work with customers to identify issues, report them and follow through. We also train testing professionals and build tools for problems encountered in our own work.

Trust means working closely with customers and partners. Quality means applying standards and identifying shortcomings before they affect users. Innovation means investing in practical tools such as Q-Bot and Q-Guard.
