    And03 / 07

    Usable by everyone. Required by law, too.

    The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025. We check your web and mobile services against WCAG and the French RGAA, and explain every barrier we find.

    1. 01

      Automated checks

      A first pass over every page to catch what tools can find.

    2. 02

      Manual review

      Keyboard, screen readers, zoom and contrast, checked by a person.

    3. 03

      Report

      Each barrier with the criterion it fails and how to fix it.
