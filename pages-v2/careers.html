Testing profiles

Different ways to work in quality.

  • 01

    Test Analyst

    Defines acceptance strategies, tests, reports anomalies and follows fixes from the start of a project.

  • 02

    Test Lead

    Implements test strategies and tracks indicators on projects or within agile teams.

  • 03

    Test Manager

    Anticipates quality risks, steers test teams and supports audits and quality assurance.

  • 04

    Test Automation

    Combines testing and development to automate functional and performance tests and track execution.
