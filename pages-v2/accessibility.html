Accessibility assessment

An assessment grounded in standards

Q-Leap combines automated checks with manual WCAG reviews and reports aligned with RGAA. Testing covers web, native and hybrid applications, including navigation, forms, contrast and compatibility.

The European Accessibility Act applies to covered products and services from 28 June 2025. Read the EU summary.

  1. Automated checks

    Identify detectable barriers with automated tools.

  2. Manual review

    Review journeys and controls against accessibility standards.

  3. Findings and recommendations

    Receive a report to help prioritise corrections.
