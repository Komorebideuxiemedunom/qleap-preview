How we test

Follow one release through our method. Your users will never meet these defects.

Demo scenario on a fictional booking app. The method is ours, the app and the figures are illustrations. Skip the demo

  1. 01 · Needs analysis

    Before a single test runs, we read what the release has to do.

    Specifications, user stories, regulations, the things everyone assumes. Three of them for this release:

    • R1 A booking can be completed with the keyboard alone.
    • R2 Search answers in under 2 seconds with 500 people online.
    • R3 One payment creates exactly one booking.
  2. 02 · Planning

    Each requirement becomes a test, with the risk written next to it.

    Accessibility, performance and functional checks go into one plan, ranked by what would hurt your users most.

    • TC-01 Keyboard path to Confirm. Accessibility, WCAG 2.1.1.
    • TC-02 Search at 500 concurrent users. Performance.
    • TC-03 Back button after payment. Functional.
  3. 03 · Execution

    Then we run them. By hand where judgement matters, automated where repetition does.

    • TC-01 failed Focus jumps from Seats to Terms. Confirm cannot be reached.
    • TC-02 failed 4.8 s at the 95th percentile with 500 users.
    • TC-03 failed Going back after payment books the seats twice.
  4. 04 · Report

    A defect is only useful if your developers can fix it on Monday.

    Each issue comes with what we expected, what we observed, how to reproduce it and what it costs your users. After the fix, we test again.

    Three defects found. Three fixed and re-tested. Release 4.2 goes live and nobody notices a thing. That is the job.

Testing services

Pick the test that matches the risk.

We join your project as a dedicated tester or a whole team, on site or from our office, from the first specification to go-live.

  • 01

    Functional testing

    The main features do what the specification says, checked by hand or by script.

  • 02

    Barriers found and explained against WCAG and the French RGAA, before an audit or a complaint finds them.

  • 03

    Performance

    Speed, stability and capacity under the load of a real launch day.

  • 04

    Regression suites that run every night, so people test what needs a human.

    Your business users confirm the software meets the agreed requirements before production.
